01 / THE STORY
Some secrets refuse to stay hidden.

When a young lawyer finds himself caught in a web of secrets and unanswered questions, the search for truth becomes a journey into the darker side of human nature. Every answer raises another question, and every revelation brings him closer to a truth he may wish had stayed buried.

Because sometimes, all it takes is one call.

And if you ever call, will someone still answer?
