01 / THE STORY
Some secrets refuse to stay hidden.

A MURDER. A CONFESSION. A TRUTH THAT REFUSES TO STAY BURIED.

Some questions are better left unanswered. Some secrets refuse to stay hidden.

When a young lawyer finds himself caught in a web of unanswered questions and dangerous secrets, the search for truth becomes more than a pursuit of justice. It becomes a journey into the shadows of human nature, where every revelation raises another question, every confession hides something more, and the truth may be far more unsettling than the crime itself.

As the pieces begin to fall into place, one question remains: how far would you go to uncover a truth that could change everything?

Because sometimes, all it takes is one call.

And if you ever call, will someone still answer?

The person behind the pages

About the Author

If You Ever Call marks Bhaiya Aware’s debut as a novelist, introducing a storyteller drawn to the uneasy spaces between truth, memory, and the secrets people keep.

With a fascination for human nature and the choices that shape us, he writes stories where every answer opens another door and the truth is rarely as simple as it first appears. In his debut novel, suspense and intimate questions of trust and justice come together in a story that asks what we are willing to uncover, and what we might wish had stayed hidden.

For Bhaiya, a story does not end at the final page. It stays with you, quietly asking its questions long after you have closed the book.
